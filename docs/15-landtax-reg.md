## 4. Exemptions (Article 5 — ຍົກເວັ້ນ)

The decree's word for waiver is **ຍົກເວັ້ນ** — the same word our UI uses for
the Waive action. Legal grounds as read from the scan:

**Agricultural / forest land:**
1. Land used for research or seed/variety production
2. Newly created irrigated land — **exempt 3 years** from the first year
3. Paddy/field hit by disaster with damage **≥ 70% — exempt the following year**
4. Registered orchards with **≥ 1,100 trees/ha** — exempt
5. Degraded land restored (reforestation / animal husbandry) — **exempt 3 years**
6. Households hit by house fires — the year's tax is **considered for waiver**

**Construction land:**
1. Land occupied by disabled veterans / surviving martyrs — up to
   8 m² per family member exempt (per Land Law art. 42); the remainder is payable
2. Premises of state agencies: non-military research, defence/security
   facilities, schools, hospitals, stadiums, public parks
3. Temples, stupas, cemeteries and other religious sites of all faiths
4. Licensed private schools
5. Embassies and international organisations (per the Ministry of Foreign
   Affairs, international practice)
6. Foreigners / stateless persons who **lease** (not own) land — do not pay

## 5. Surcharges (Article 10)

| Case | Surcharge |
|---|---|
| Declared & paid late (past the due date of Art. 7) | 10% of the year's tax |
| Declared but incomplete / wrong | 30% of the year's tax |
| Refuses to declare — 1st year | back tax + 100% |
| — 2nd year | back tax + 150% + written warning |
| — 3rd year | back tax + 200% |
| After 3 years | loss of usage right (Land Law art. 62(d)) |
| Idle production land (unused for a year) | written warning; year 1: +50%, year 2: +100%, year 3: +110% of land value `[verify]` |
| Irrigated paddy left uncultivated | +50% of the paddy tax |
| Obstructing tax officials | +50% + criminal prosecution |
| Officials who mis-collect / keep the money | discipline + full refund + prosecution |

**Tax year: 1 October – 30 September** (state budget year).

**Implemented — 10% only.** The app applies the first row: an obligation that is
still `UNPAID`/`PARTIAL` after `fee_obligations.due_date` carries
`ROUND(amount_due × 0.10, 2)`. It is derived live (`fee_penalty_sql()` in
`lib/fee.php`) and frozen into `fee_obligations.penalty` when the obligation is
settled. An officer may waive or reduce it with a reason (`penalty_override`,
`penalty_note`, audited as `obligation_penalty`). The remaining rows — 30% wrong
declaration, refusal 100/150/200%, idle land, uncultivated paddy, obstruction —
are **not** implemented.

## 6. Reconciliation with our seed rates — off by ~3 orders of magnitude

The original placeholder seed shipped 248 tier rates in the band
**85,000 – 180,000 kip/m²/year** (main 180k / branch 140k / alley 110k /
no-road 85k). The decree's construction-land rates are
**5 – 300 kip/m²/year**:

| | Ours (seed) | Decree 385/2009 | Factor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lowest tier (no-road) | 85,000 kip/m²/yr | max in the whole table: 300 | **×283 above the legal maximum** |
| Highest tier (main road) | 180,000 kip/m²/yr | 300 | ×600 |
| Rural village (decree) | still 85k+ | 5 – 45 | ×1,900 – ×36,000 |

Worked example: a 100 m² plot in a provincial town centre (class II,
centre row) — decree: 60–250 kip/m²/yr → **6,000–25,000 kip/year**
(≈ US$0.25–1.10/yr). The app quoted **8.5–18 million kip/year**
(≈ US$360–800/yr) before this fix.

**Conclusion:** the numbers we picked for the original seed band were not
annual land tax — their magnitude matched **one-off land-transaction /
land-use fees** (paid per m² at transfer/registration). The annual land tax
is a few hundred kip per m² at most.

### Applied mapping (decision made — decree band is now live in the seed)

The decree is from **2009** and Art. 8 delegates rate adjustment to the
Cabinet "to match the economic situation" — a newer table may be in force.
Decision: **re-base the system on the decree now**; the province can always
override it later, either by re-running an updated seed or (the supported
path) editing values in **Basic Data > Zone rates**. No app or code change
is needed for that — the fee engine only reads the `zone_rate_tiers` /
`zones` tables.

Implementation:
- `sql/seed_zone_rates_savannakhet.sql` — fresh installs now create the 62
  zones with decree-band tier rates (248 rows, 3.5–250 kip/m²/yr)
- `sql/migrations/011_decree385_zone_rates.sql` — UPDATE-only, idempotent:
  re-bases existing seed-zone tiers + `zones.rate_per_sqm` fallbacks and the
  legacy unassigned zones (flat 60 kip/m²/yr). Apply by hand on the server;
  it overwrites any province figures already entered in Basic Data, so
  re-enter those afterwards if they differ
- `sql/seed_savannakhet.sql` — the reference-only `zones_rates` rows follow
  the same band
